What changed in each version of 454 Workshop, newest first. The version you have is shown in
**About 454 Workshop**, in the menu, and beside each app's name in its header.

Changes that affect how the machine moves or cuts are marked **(cutting)**. After an update with one of those,
air-cut your next job before cutting material.

The full record, in more detail than most people want, is the
[change log on GitHub](https://github.com/ctredway/454-workshop/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.6.2-beta.63

Not released yet

- **History steps can follow parameters.** In 454 Design, a step's size (an offset's distance, a fillet's radius,
  an array's count, spacing or angle) can be a parameter or a formula; change the parameter and the step and
  everything after it are worked out again.
- **Array is on the History bar,** and can be changed later like the other steps.
- **An easier History bar.** An icon instead of the word; a marker you drag to go back and forward through the
  steps; and right-click a step to go to it, change it, or delete it. When you've gone back, the right-click menu
  can remove all the steps after the marker.
- **Toolpaths left out of a run show faint.** In 454 Control's preview, the toolpaths you untick don't stand out
  as if they'll be cut.

## 0.6.2-beta.62

22 October 2026

- **Changing the material thickness shows the Working panel.** In 454 Design, a new thickness in Job setup works
  out the toolpaths that follow it again, one at a time, with the panel showing which, instead of looking frozen.
- **Fixed: with Z zero at the bottom of the material, a new thickness left most toolpaths at their old heights.**
  Only through cuts were worked out again, and Save G-code would have written the others for the old top of the
  material. Every toolpath now follows the thickness when Z zero is at the bottom. **(cutting)**
- **Fixed: Pause didn't lift.** In 454 Control's run bar, **Pause & lift** now stops the machine and the spindle and
  lifts Z to the top, on any Shapeoko (the old button relied on a firmware feature stock Shapeokos don't have).
  **Resume** brings the bit back down where it stopped and carries on, with the same adjustments; **End job**
  finishes instead. **(cutting)** Try it with an air cut first.
- **Fixed: Z +6 mm in the jog panel did nothing.** It now lifts 6 mm from where Z is (less near the top).
- **A clearer Adjust panel.** Four labelled rows, each showing what it's at now: Feed rate, Spindle speed, Rapid
  moves and Depth nudge (▼ deeper, ▲ shallower), with the units on every button.

## 0.6.2-beta.61

22 October 2026

- **Fixed: a toolpath ran very slowly after left-out toolpaths or a tool change.** A toolpath at the same feed as
  the one before has no feed of its own in the file, and the BitSetter's slow touch left the machine at 40 mm/min.
  454 Control now always sets the file's feed again. **(cutting)**
- **Fixed: files in inches.** 454 Control's own moves (lifting, parking, the tool-change position, the BitSetter)
  are in millimetres, but in or after a file in inches the machine read them as inches: a lift stopped far short
  of the top, and parking could ask for a spot far past the end of the machine. They now always run in
  millimetres, and the file's own units, feed and modes are put back after them and after every tool change.
  **(cutting)** Air-cut your next inch job.
- **The Windows icon shows the whole logo**, not just the 5, on the taskbar and the desktop.

## 0.6.2-beta.60

22 October 2026

- **Run only some of a file's toolpaths.** In 454 Control's Toolpaths tab, untick **Run** on the toolpaths to
  leave out, and Start runs the rest: no need to go back to Design and save the G-code again. Before each
  toolpath after a left-out one, the machine lifts, changes the tool if the left-out one loaded it, goes to where
  the toolpath starts and starts the spindle. **(cutting)** Air-cut the first time, and check that a left-out
  toolpath wasn't the one that cut a part free.

## 0.6.2-beta.59

22 October 2026

- **Change tool measures the new bit.** In 454 Control, Change tool in the jog panel went to the tool-change
  position and then offered only **Done**, without measuring, if the BitSetter hadn't measured the bit when Z zero
  was set (answering **Later**, or reconnecting, left it without one). Now it measures the bit that's in first, with
  the spindle stopped, then parks, then measures the new bit, so your Z zero is kept. **(cutting)**
- **Dimensions can be dragged out of the way.** In 454 Design, with the Select tool, drag a dimension's value. A
  distance's line moves out to the side, with thin lines back to the edges it measures, and its value slides along
  it; a diameter or radius can go anywhere, with a thin line back to its circle. It stays where you put it when
  the shapes move. Esc during the drag puts it back, and Undo undoes a move.

## 0.6.2-beta.58

22 October 2026

- **Handles as in VCarve.** In 454 Design, selecting a shape shows its scale handles straight away. Click the
  selected shape again for rotate handles (curved arrows at the corners, and a centre you can drag); click again
  to go back to scale. A double-click on text opens it for editing.
- **Midpoints you can see.** While drawing, the pointer snaps to the middle of every side: lines, rectangle sides,
  polyline and outline segments (the middle of a curve) and arcs. A triangle and the word **Midpoint** show when
  it does; ends, corners and centres keep the small square.

## 0.6.2-beta.57

20 October 2026

- **A change made back in the history keeps the steps after it.** In 454 Design, click an earlier step on the
  History bar and change something: the change goes in there, as in Fusion, and the steps after it stay. Come
  forward to them (click a later step, or Redo) and they're done again on top of your change. One that can't be,
  because its shapes aren't there any more, turns red and is skipped. They used to be dropped.

## 0.6.2-beta.56

20 October 2026

- **The port chooser names your machine.** Connecting in 454 Control, each port is listed by its name, such as
  **Carbide 3D Shapeoko 5**, not just a path like `/dev/ttyACM0`. The one that looks like your machine is first,
  marked, and already chosen. On Linux, the ports built into the computer, never the machine, are out of the way
  under **Show all ports**, and a long list scrolls with **Cancel** always in view.

## 0.6.2-beta.55

20 October 2026

- **A moved curved outline is drawn where it is.** An outline with curves in it, such as one made with Join, was
  still drawn where it had been after it was moved (dragged, nudged, pasted, centred or nested), though it had moved,
  and its holes went with it. Its toolpaths followed where it really was. If you moved one before this update, close
  and open the drawing to see it in its true place, and check its toolpaths.

## 0.6.2-beta.54

20 October 2026

- **A Tools menu.** In 454 Design the menu bar now has **Tools**, after View, with every tool on the toolbar in the
  toolbar's own groups: **Draw**, **Change shapes**, **Move and copy**, **Combine**, **Text** and **Align and nest**,
  then Dimension, Measure, Parameters and the guides. Each does what its button does, and shows its key.

## 0.6.2-beta.53

20 October 2026

- **The Edit menu edits the drawing.** In 454 Design, **Edit** now has **Undo**, **Redo**, **Cut**, **Copy**,
  **Paste**, **Paste in place**, **Duplicate**, **Delete**, **Select all**, **Group** and **Ungroup**, each doing what
  its key does. Before, Edit → Undo didn't undo a drawing step, and Select all selected the page's text. While you're
  typing in a box, Cut, Copy, Paste, Delete and Select all still work on the text.

## 0.6.2-beta.52

20 October 2026

- **Jog with a game controller.** An Xbox-style controller, USB or Bluetooth, as a pendant at the machine: the sticks
  jog while held, the D-pad steps, B stops. Switch it on in **Settings → Accessories**. It only ever jogs or stops:
  it can't start a job, set zero or run the spindle. See [Game controller](control-reference.html#game-controller).

## 0.6.2-beta.51

19 October 2026

- **The History bar takes less room:** each step is its tool's icon (a rectangle, a fillet, an offset), and pointing
  at one shows its name, when it was made, and why it was skipped if it's red.

## 0.6.2-beta.50

19 October 2026

- **The History bar,** along the bottom of the drawing: every change you make is a step, named for what it did
  (Rectangle, Fillet 6 mm, Offset 3 mm, Weld). Click a step to see the drawing as it was just after it, and a later
  one to come forward again. The history is saved in the drawing's file. See [History](design-workspace.html#history).
- **Change a step, as in Fusion:** double-click it, change its numbers (a rectangle's size, a fillet's radius, an
  offset's distance, how far a move went), and every step after it is done again. Fillets, offsets, mirrors, welds,
  joins, trims and moves follow the change; toolpaths cut the shapes as they now are. A step that can't be done again
  turns red and is skipped, never guessed at. Undo takes a change back.
- **A new or opened drawing starts its own history:** the drawing that was open is in its file, or put aside for
  Recover.

## 0.6.2-beta.49

19 October 2026

- **The Inlay wizard.** Select the shapes, press **+** and choose **Inlay**: choose the carving bit (a V-bit or a
  tapered ball nose) and the end mill, set the depths, and Create makes both halves, the pocket and the plug,
  each in a group, with the plug on a sheet of its own. See [Inlay](cam-reference.html#inlay).
- **The + key** makes a new toolpath for the shapes selected, as the + at the top of the Toolpaths panel does.
- **A Working panel** while toolpaths are worked out, so the app never looks frozen: it says which toolpath it's
  on, with a bar. Control shows "Loading…" while a big G-code file loads.
- **The toolpath editor works nothing out until you press Create or Update,** as in VCarve, so typing never lags.
  Create says so if nothing is selected, or if the shapes can't be cut (open lines for a pocket), and which were
  left out.
- **Undo and redo are quick again** on big projects: toolpaths already worked out are used again, not worked out
  from scratch. Opening a drawing, and layer and sheet changes, are quicker for the same reason.

## 0.6.2-beta.48

19 October 2026

- **(cutting) V-carving with a tapered ball nose.** A tapered ball nose from the tool library is now carved as what
  it is, a cone ending in a small ball. Before, it was carved as a sharp V-bit of the same angle, which on a bit
  that narrow is far too deep: a 2 mm wide line came out 10.6 mm deep, not 2.9 mm. Inlays work with one too.
  **A V-carve made before with a tapered ball nose takes its ball when it's next recalculated: check its depth
  before cutting.**
- **(cutting) Fixed: flat-tipped and tapered bits cut past the walls** where a V-carve climbs into a corner of an
  outline made of short straight pieces (text, imported and traced shapes), by up to 0.7 mm. Sharp V-bits were
  never affected and haven't changed.
- **Fixed: Preview in wood showed V-carves shallower than they're cut,** at corners and down slopes, by
  millimetres with a narrow bit. It now shows them as cut.

## 0.6.2-beta.47

19 October 2026

- **Fillet corners.** Select some shapes and round every corner at once, or only the corners pointing out or in,
  with round fillets, dog-bones or T-bones. Any corner the radius won't fit is ringed in red and left sharp. See
  [Fillet corners](design-tools.html#fillet-corners).
- **Toolpath groups.** Ctrl+click toolpath cards and group them: a group folds to one line, its toolpaths are
  ticked, shown and recalculated together, it moves through the cutting order as one, and it can be saved as a
  G-code file of its own. See [Groups](cam-reference.html#groups).
- **Choosing a material in the tool library:** click it in a tool's Feeds and speeds list. The Material
  dropdown is gone.

## 0.6.2-beta.46

18 October 2026

- **454 Workshop for Linux,** as an AppImage: one file that runs without installing, on 64-bit Intel and AMD
  computers, and keeps itself up to date like the Windows version. See
  [Installing on Linux](desktop-app.html#installing-on-linux). It's new: it has run jobs against the simulated
  machine, not yet at a real one.
- Nothing has changed in the Windows version.

## 0.6.2-beta.45

17 October 2026

- **(machine) The computer stays awake while the machine is busy:** a job, a probe, a jog or a quick action
  running, or the spindle on. A computer that went to sleep partway through a job stopped feeding the machine
  with the spindle running. The screen can still turn off. In a browser, Control keeps the screen on during a job.
- **(machine) 454 Control's 3D view is drawn only when it changes,** not 60 times a second. On a computer without
  graphics acceleration, drawing it starved a long job of lines, leaving the machine waiting mid-cut.
- **Change tool…** in the jog panel, as in Carbide Motion: the machine lifts, stops the spindle, goes to the
  tool-change position and waits. With a BitSetter, it then measures the new bit and keeps your Z zero.
- **A tidier Machine tab,** in the order you work: Machine, Set up (Jog & zero, Probe Z, Spindle), Run job, Quick
  actions. The spindle controls and the console open in windows of their own, and the jog keys stand out.

## 0.6.2-beta.44

5 October 2026

- **(cutting) The V-bit flattens the floor its clearing cutter can't reach.** An end mill can't get into the
  corners of a V-carve's flat floor, or into parts narrower than itself. The V-bit now goes back over those
  places at the floor's depth, as VCarve's does, so an inlay seats. It's for a V-carve with its own clearing
  pocket, an inlay made from now on, and inlay toolpaths from a VCarve project. **Make older inlays again to get
  it.** The note about floor left high, added in beta.43, is gone: there's nothing left to warn about.
- **(cutting) Fixed: a pocket with a stepover over 50% could leave a thin strip standing** down the middle of a
  channel, where the rings from the two sides didn't quite meet. The middle is now cut. Pockets at 50% or less
  (the default is 40%) were never affected and haven't changed.
- **V-carves and pockets with islands calculate much faster.** A V-carve over many shapes that took 14 seconds
  now takes a fraction of one, and pockets with islands are about four times quicker. The toolpaths are exactly
  the same.

## 0.6.2-beta.43

4 October 2026

- **(cutting) Fixed: updating an inlay plug's V-carve lost its start depth.** Opening the plug's V-carve and
  pressing Update made its walls begin at the surface, so the plug came out small and loose. It now cuts as it
  did when made. **If you've edited an inlay plug's V-carve, make the inlay again.**
- **Fixed: choosing a clearing pocket's cutter unlinked it from its V-carve,** so a later change to the
  V-carve's max depth added a second pocket. The one pocket now follows.
- **A V-carve's clearing pocket says when it leaves floor.** An end mill can't get into the corners of a
  V-carve's flat floor, or into parts narrower than itself, and 454's V-bit doesn't flatten them. The clearing
  toolpath now says so on its card, with how high that floor is left. An inlay won't seat on it: use a smaller
  cutter, or pare it flat.

## 0.6.2-beta.42

4 October 2026

- **(cutting) Fixed: VCarve toolpaths made editable had wrong depths** in projects in inches (a 0.26 in cut came
  across 0.26 mm deep), and pockets from newer VCarve came across 3 mm deep whatever their depth. Both now get
  their real depth, with the project's own cutter size and feeds. A toolpath whose depth can't be read isn't
  converted. **If you've made a VCarve project's toolpaths editable before, make them editable again.**
- **VCarve inlay toolpaths come across:** the V-carve, with its start depth and the V-bit's real angle, and the
  clearing pocket that goes with it. Toolpaths that use a group or a block of text find their shapes, and each
  toolpath comes across once, under its own name.
- **Fixed: a lone line or arc in a VCarve project didn't come across,** and open shapes were closed. Both now
  open as VCarve has them.

  spot. Text now opens where VCarve has it, including text on a curve.
- **Fixed: a VCarve project zeroed at the centre of its material opened at half its size,** with no

## 0.6.2-beta.37

2 October 2026

- **Open and Import are separate.** Open (Ctrl+O) opens a drawing or a VCarve or Carbide Create project; Import
  (Ctrl+I) brings DXF or SVG vectors, or a picture to trace, into the drawing that's open.
- **A tidier File group** with the usual icons (a folder to open, a floppy disk to save), and Undo, Redo, Fit stock
  and Measure moved into it from View & History.
- **The left panel folds away** for more drawing room: **«** at the bottom of its Tools and Layers tabs. Click
  **»** or a tab to open it again.
- **Fixed: a toolpath hidden and shown again had no tabs on the drawing** until it was updated. Every toolpath
  shown now shows its tabs. (They were always in the G-code.)
- **Fixed: Trim.** Rectangles can be trimmed, shapes in a group (an imported DXF or SVG) stop a trim where they
  cross, and trimming a closed shape no longer sometimes removes the wrong piece.
- **Fixed: New kept the old drawing's sheets.** A new drawing now starts with one sheet, and without the old
  drawing's parameters or name.

## 0.6.2-beta.36

2 October 2026

- **A File menu.** The desktop app's menu bar is now the usual File, Edit, View, Window and Help. File has New,
  Open, Save, Save As, the exports and Save G-code in Design, and Open G-code file in Control; Help has the help,
  the docs, updates and About.
- **See every pass.** The toolpath editor now shows how many passes a profile or pocket takes and the depth of
  each one, as you change the depth or Per pass. Type a number of passes to divide the depth evenly.
- **Fixed: some cuts went round the final depth twice.** For sizes such as 3/4 in deep at 1/4 in a pass, the
  last pass was repeated at the same depth. It wasted time and cut nothing extra; it's gone.

## 0.6.2-beta.33

2 October 2026

- **Fixed: the eye on a toolpath's card** now hides and shows that toolpath on the drawing. Before, the icon

- **Pictures in the toolpath editor's help.** Every kind of cut, and Stepover, Ramp in, Lead in/out and Tabs,

## 0.6.2-beta.32

2 October 2026

- **Name your toolpaths.** Type a name in the toolpath editor, or double-click the name on a card. It shows on
  the card and the job sheet and in the G-code. See
  [the Toolpaths panel](cam-reference.html).
- **A roomier toolpath editor.** It now takes over the Toolpaths panel while it's open, with its settings
  under headings and the drawing still in view. Click any setting and a help box says what it does.
- **Fold toolpath cards to their titles,** one at a time or all at once, so a long list fits on the screen. A
  folded card still shows ⚠ when its toolpath has a warning.
- **Fixed:** a toolpath whose cut was changed (outside to inside, say) kept its old name.

## 0.6.2-beta.31

1 October 2026

- **Open Carbide Create projects (.c2d),** from current and older versions. The shapes, text, layers, material
  and XY zero come across, in the project's units. Contours, pockets and V-carves from projects saved by older
  Carbide Create come in as editable 454 toolpaths; what can't be converted for certain is left out and named.
  Check every toolpath, and air-cut, before cutting. See the
  [Design quick start](design-quickstart.html#open-a-carbide-create-project).
- **Fixed: a Z nudge stayed on after its job.** Nudging Z in Control's Adjust panel during a job shifted the
  next job by the same amount, with the panel reading Z +0.00. A nudge now ends when its job does. If you
  nudged in an earlier version and haven't set Z zero since, set it again before cutting.
- **Fixed: nudge buttons pressed quickly** could leave an older nudge in force than the one shown.
- **Keep for the next job.** Every job starts with feed, spindle and rapid at 100% and no Z nudge. A new tick
  box in the Adjust panel carries them into the next job instead, which asks before it starts. See
  [Adjusting a running job](control-reference.html#running-a-job).

## 0.6.2-beta.30

1 October 2026

- **Add sheets to a project.** A project that needs more than one piece of material can have a sheet for each.
  The **+** in the Sheet bar, at the top of the Toolpaths panel, adds one; the pencil renames it and the bin
  deletes it. Each sheet has its own shapes, toolpaths and G-code file. Before, Design could only show the
  sheets of a VCarve project. See [Sheets](design-workspace.html#sheets).
- **Fixed: a multi-sheet project drew every sheet's toolpaths on top of each other (cutting).** Only the
  shown sheet's toolpaths are drawn now. The G-code was always one sheet's.
- Trim, Extend, Fillet, Nest, Check and the DXF and SVG exports now work on the sheet being shown only.

## 0.6.2-beta.29

1 October 2026

- **Messages now say "Job setup"** where they used to say "Settings". The material's thickness is set in Job
  setup (the material icon, top right of 454 Design), and about ten messages sent you to Settings instead.
- **Fixed: a button that did nothing.** Saving G-code for a through cut with no material thickness set shows
  "Set the material thickness first". Its button now opens Job setup. Before, it did nothing.
- The docs have this page, pictures of parameters, the depth warning and the start point, and a parameters
  section in the Design quick start.

## 0.6.2-beta.28

30 September 2026

- **Parameters.** Name a size once (`thickness` = 18) with the **fx** button, use the name in your dimensions,
  and change it in one place: every dimension using it follows. Arithmetic works too: `600 - 2 * thickness`.
  See [Parameters](design-tools.html#parameters).
- **Toolpath depths and tabs can use parameters**, such as a pocket `material / 2` deep. They are recalculated
  when the number changes.
- **Design warns when a cut would go past the bottom of the material (cutting).** Any toolpath that does is
  marked ⚠ on its card, and Save G-code stops and names it first. Before this, only V-carves warned.
- **Every length box takes arithmetic.** Type `12 + 3/4` or `600 - 2 * thickness` where you'd type a number.
