The desktop app is 454 Design, 454 Control and these docs in one program for Windows, with no browser
needed and nothing loaded from the internet. It talks to the machine directly over USB.

:::note
The desktop app is in testing. **Download for Windows** on the [home page](/) gets the newest version. All of
them are on the project's
<a href="https://github.com/ctredway/454-workshop/releases" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GitHub Releases page<span class="sr-only"> (opens in a new tab)</span></a>,
newest at the top.
:::

## Installing

Each release has two downloads (under **Assets** on the Releases page):

- **The installer**, named like `454-Workshop-0.6.2-beta.26-win-x64.exe`: installs 454 Workshop for your user account, adds it to the Start menu, and
  **keeps it up to date**. This is the one to use.
- **The zip**: runs from wherever you unzip it, with nothing installed. It can't update itself, so it's best
  kept for trying a build out.

Your settings, tool library and drawings are kept separately from the program, so they carry over between
versions, and between a zip copy and the installed one.

:::tip
Windows may warn that the installer is from an unrecognised publisher, because it isn't signed yet. Choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.
:::

**Reinstalling over an older copy?** Quit 454 Workshop first, and check Task Manager that nothing called
454 Workshop is still running. Then launch it from the **Start menu**: an old pinned shortcut may still
point at a copy you unzipped earlier. **454 Workshop → About 454 Workshop** shows which version you're
running.

## Updates

The installed app checks for updates 30 seconds after it starts, then every 30 minutes while it's open (for now, while beta releases come often).

Nothing pops up. When there's a new version, a notice appears at the top of 454 Control and 454 Design,
beside **Docs**, and nothing more happens until you click it.

<Steps>

1. **Update available**: click it to see what's new, then choose **Download** or **Skip this version**.
   To decide later, just leave it. Every version's changes are listed on [What's new](whats-new.html).
2. **Downloading update**: it downloads in the background while you work, and the notice shows how far it
   has got. The download is checked against the release before it's used. If it doesn't match, or the
   download fails, the notice says **Update didn't download**: click it to see why, and to **Try again**.
3. **Restart to update**: click it, then **Restart now**. 454 Control and 454 Design close, the update
   installs, and 454 Workshop opens again. The machine disconnects, so reconnect it afterwards. If 454
   Design has a drawing that isn't saved to a file, it asks first: **Save…**, **Don't save** (it can be
   recovered) or **Cancel** (which stops the restart). Or leave it: the update installs the next time you
   close 454 Workshop.

</Steps>

:::caution[Never during a job]
While 454 Control is busy (running a job, probing, jogging or running a quick action, with the spindle on,
or with the machine moving), **Restart to update** turns grey and **Restart now** can't be pressed. 454
Workshop checks again when you press it, so if the machine gets busy just before, it doesn't restart. The
download itself never interrupts anything.
:::

In the **Help** menu:

- **Check for updates…** checks now, and tells you either way in a window: if there's a new version, the
  same **Download**, **Later** or **Skip this version** choice. Once an update is downloaded, it becomes
  **Restart to update to…**. The questions it asks wait until the machine is idle.
- **Updates → Check automatically** turns the background checks on or off.
- **Updates → Stable releases** or **Beta: pre-releases too** chooses the channel. Beta also offers
  pre-release versions: early, and meant for testing. A beta version starts on the Beta channel, so it's
  offered the next beta; a stable version starts on Stable. Once you choose, your choice is kept.

A copy run from a zip explains that it can't update itself, and how to get one that can.

## The menus

The menu bar is the usual one: **File**, **Edit**, **View**, **Window** and **Help**.

- **File** follows the window in front. In 454 Design it has **New**, **Open…**, **Save**, **Save As…** and
  **Recover last drawing**; **Export DXF…**, **Export SVG…** and **Save G-code…**; and **Job setup…** and
  **Settings…**. In 454 Control it has **Open G-code file…** and **Settings…**. **Close window** and **Exit**
  are always at the bottom. Each item does exactly what its button in the window does, and, like the keys,
  nothing while a dialog is open.
- **Window** switches between **454 Design** and **454 Control**, and ticks the one in front.
- **Help** has **Help** (the guides, in a window over your work), **Docs** (the guides in a window of their
  own), **Report a problem…**, the update items above, and **About 454 Workshop**.
