454 Control needs Chrome or Edge

To run your machine, open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (or another browser built on Chromium, such as Opera) on a computer, or use the 454 Workshop desktop app.

You can still continue here to open and check G-code files; you just can’t connect to a machine.

Connect to your machine?

454 Control can open and check G-code on its own, but it needs a connection to jog, probe or cut.

Home the machine?

Homing sets the machine’s own coordinates. Until it runs, machine positions mean nothing, so the BitSetter, park moves, job recovery and anything using G53 can’t be trusted.

The machine will move to its limit switches. Check the spindle is clear and your hand is near the stop.

New quick action

Comments in (parentheses) or after a ; are ignored. G53 moves use machine coordinates, so they go to the same place every time once the machine is homed.
