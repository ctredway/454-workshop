Parts bin maker

A tray with drop-in inserts, for 3D printing. Mix inserts of different lengths in the same tray, and stack the trays: each one sits in the rim of the one below. Set it up here, check the preview, then download a 3MF or STL file for your slicer (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Orca, Cura).

Printing it

  • Every part is flat side down, ready to slice. In a 3MF each part is its own object, laid out on the plate; print them as they are.
  • The parts need no supports. PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, is a good start.
  • Print one insert first to check the fit, and change the Clearance if you need to.
